Jan – Sep 2025

Independent research in computational plasma physics, supervised by Professor Colin Cotter. Developed finite element solvers for the collisionless Vlasov–Poisson system using Firedrake, implementing discontinuous Galerkin discretisations, Hermite quadrature and SSPRK3 time integration to investigate hybrid multistream formulations for kinetic plasma simulation. Combined finite element methods, numerical analysis and computational PDEs, with convergence studies, moment-based error analysis, and numerical validation against full phase-space finite element models. Full write-up and code on Research →.
